PhotoClean is a photo cleanup tool that runs entirely on your device. Our core principle is simple: your photos and data never leave it.

1We collect no data

This app does not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell any personal information about you. Specifically, it never:

  • collects your name, email, phone number, or any identifying information
  • collects your photos, videos, or any file contents
  • collects device information such as your device model or OS version
  • uses any third-party analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry SDK
  • uses advertising identifiers (such as IDFA or GAID) or requests cross-app tracking permission
  • builds a profile of you or performs behavioural analysis
  • shares data with any data broker, ad network, or third party

2Everything happens on-device

Every core feature — similar photo detection, blur detection, screenshot cleanup, and video compression — runs entirely on your device.

  • Photos and videos are never uploaded to any server
  • No cloud AI or remote compute service is used
  • Every feature works without a network connection
  • We operate no servers, so your data never passes through us

3Storage and deletion

This app stores the following on your device only:

  • App settings, such as the similarity threshold and language preference
  • Local caches that speed up later scans, such as image feature vectors
  • Subscription status, managed by the app store; we never see payment details

This data is stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

4Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play.

  • We never touch or store your card details or store account
  • Subscription status is verified and managed by the respective store
  • Each store's handling of payment data is governed by that store's own privacy policy

5System permissions

This app requests the following permissions:

  • Photos and videos (read): to scan for similar, blurry, and screenshot photos — processed on-device, never uploaded
  • Photos and videos (write): to save compressed videos to your library — processed on-device, never uploaded

If you decline, the core features will not work, but no data is transmitted off your device.

6Not for children

This app is not designed for children under 13. Because it collects no user data whatsoever, there is no children's personal information for which parental consent would be required.

7Your rights

Because we collect and store no data about you, the rights below are fully realised by deleting the app:

  • Access: you can review all local data this app stores, on your device
  • Deletion: deleting the app deletes all local data
  • Withdrawal: you can revoke photo library access any time in your device's system settings

8Policy updates

If we change how the app works — for example by adding a cloud feature — we will update this policy before the change takes effect and notify you in-app or through the store release notes. Continuing to use the updated app means you accept the updated policy.

9Contact

If you have any question about this policy, contact us:

Email: volleychzm@gmail.com

隐私政策

PhotoClean · 生效日期 2026-10-09 · 版本 1.0 · 适用于 Android 应用 com.photoclean.app 与 iOS 应用 PhotoClean

PhotoClean 是一款完全在设备本地运行的照片整理工具。我们设计它的核心原则是：你的照片和数据永远不会离开你的设备。

1我们不收集任何数据

本应用不收集、不存储、不传输、不分享、不出售你的任何个人信息。具体而言，本应用不会：

  • 收集你的姓名、邮箱、电话号码或任何身份标识信息
  • 收集你的照片、视频或任何文件内容
  • 收集你的设备信息（如设备型号、系统版本等）
  • 使用任何第三方分析、崩溃上报或遥测 SDK
  • 使用广告标识符（如 IDFA / GAID），不请求跨应用追踪授权
  • 建立用户画像或进行行为分析
  • 向任何数据代理、广告网络或第三方共享数据

2所有处理均在本地完成

本应用的全部核心功能——包括相似照片识别、模糊照片检测、截图管理、视频压缩——全部在你的设备上完成。

  • 照片和视频不会被上传到任何服务器
  • 不依赖云端 AI 或远程计算服务
  • 不需要网络连接即可使用全部功能
  • 我们不运营任何服务器，你的数据不会经过我们

3数据存储与删除

本应用仅在设备本地存储以下内容：

  • 应用设置（如相似度阈值、语言偏好）
  • 用于加速后续扫描的本地缓存（如图像特征向量）
  • 订阅状态（由应用商店的支付系统管理，我们不接触支付信息）

上述数据仅存储在你的设备上。删除本应用即可删除全部数据。

4关于订阅与支付

本应用的订阅与买断完全由 Apple App Store / Google Play 的支付系统处理。

  • 我们不接触、不存储你的支付卡信息或商店账户信息
  • 订阅状态由相应的应用商店验证和管理
  • 商店处理支付数据的方式受该商店自身的隐私政策约束

5系统权限说明

本应用会请求以下系统权限：

  • 照片和视频（读取）：扫描相似照片、模糊照片、截图，仅在设备本地处理，不上传
  • 照片和视频（写入）：保存压缩后的视频到相册，仅在设备本地处理，不上传

如果你拒绝授权，本应用的核心功能将无法使用，但不会导致任何数据被传输到外部。

6不面向儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童设计。由于本应用不收集任何用户数据，不存在需要家长同意才能收集的儿童个人信息。

7你的权利

由于我们不收集、不存储任何关于你的数据，以下权利在实践中通过「删除 App」即可完全实现：

  • 访问权：你可以在设备上查看本应用存储的所有本地数据
  • 删除权：删除 App 即删除所有本地数据
  • 撤回同意：你可以在系统设置中随时撤销照片库权限

8隐私政策更新

如果我们未来对 App 的功能做出变更（例如新增云端功能），我们会在变更生效前更新本隐私政策，并通过 App 内提示或商店更新说明告知你。你继续使用更新后的 App，即表示你接受更新后的隐私政策。
